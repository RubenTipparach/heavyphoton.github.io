# Soundtrack credits

Sound effects for the 27.6 s prison plate shop cinematic (`soundtrack.wav`).
Every sound comes from OpenGameArt.org. Each was cut, pitched, looped, filtered
and mixed in `mix.py`; the reverbs are synthetic and made in the script.

Licenses used: CC0 (public domain, no attribution required, credited anyway),
CC-BY 3.0 and CC-BY 4.0 (attribution required, given below).

## Rebuilding

`sources/` is not committed. `python3 fetch_sources.py` downloads every file
listed in `credits.json` back into it, and `python3 mix.py` rebuilds
`soundtrack.wav`.
