Heavy Photon // Logo Concepts

Three directions. Custom-built letterforms, pure SVG, zero stock. Regenerate or tweak any of them with genlogos.py.

01 — Dropship Crest

acid volt

Squadron-patch energy. A stealth delta dropship burning in under descent chevrons, locked in a chamfered shield. Reads as a sticker slap, an embroidered patch, or a loading-screen crest. The stacked justified wordmark carries the weight; PHOTON gets the volt.

02 — Visor

plasma magenta

Esports-mark DNA. A mech pilot helmet with a hot magenta visor and a glare slash, antenna up. The head works alone as an avatar/favicon at any size; the lit O in PHOTON echoes the visor so the wordmark still says "mech" even when the head is cropped out.

03 — Heavy Beam

ion cyan

The name, made literal. A chunky hand cannon fires a photon so heavy the beam sags and the slug drops off the end — strain ticks and all. It's a physics joke wearing a streetwear fit, and the beam doubles as a rule/underline for banners and wide headers.
