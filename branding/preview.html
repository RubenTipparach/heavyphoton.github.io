Heavy Photon // Logo Concepts

Three directions. Custom-built letterforms, pure SVG, zero stock. Regenerate or tweak any of them with genlogos.py.

01 — Dropship Crest

acid volt

Squadron-patch energy. A stealth delta dropship burning in under descent chevrons, locked in a chamfered shield. Reads as a sticker slap, an embroidered patch, or a loading-screen crest. The stacked justified wordmark carries the weight; PHOTON gets the volt.

02 — Visor

plasma magenta

Esports-mark DNA. A mech pilot helmet with a hot magenta visor and a glare slash, antenna up. The head works alone as an avatar/favicon at any size; the lit O in PHOTON echoes the visor so the wordmark still says "mech" even when the head is cropped out.

03 — Heavy Beam

ion cyan

The name, made literal. A chunky hand cannon fires a photon so heavy the beam sags and the slug drops off the end — strain ticks and all. It's a physics joke wearing a streetwear fit, and the beam doubles as a rule/underline for banners and wide headers.

Raygun // V2 Variants

Concept 03 pushed further: a sharper v2 alphabet (chamfered corners, blade-cut terminals, pointed A/V), accent-lit letters, no GAMES line.

03.1 — Mainline

ion cyan

The full wordmark with both O's lit cyan, riding above a beam that sags past the word under the weight of the slug it's hauling. Primary lockup pick.

03.5 — Stamped Steel

ion cyan

The mainline lockup embossed into a bolted gunmetal plate — bevel highlights and drop shadows on every structural shape, while the beam, O's, vents, and slug core stay live with a cyan glow. Splash-screen and hardware-badge material.

03.2 — Strike

plasma magenta

Beam fired straight through the name — both O's light up as nodes riding the beam, and the slug punches out the far side. Widescreen banner energy.

03.3 — Slap

acid volt

Square sticker cut: gun tilted, blasting toward the corner, stacked justified wordmark with a stencil scanline through the letters. Lamppost-ready.

03.4 — Roundel

ion cyan

Icon-only cut for avatars and favicons: gun, sagging blast, slug. No type needed once the mark is known.
